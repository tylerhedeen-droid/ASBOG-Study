Question & Progress Quality Controls

Answer positionsCorrect answers are evenly distributed across A, B, C, and D in the 580-question bank (145 each), preventing answer-letter bias.
MasteryDashboard categories are New, Learning, Strong, and Mastered. Two consecutive confidently correct attempts are required for Mastered.
Session resultsCompleted sessions show performance by both domain and difficulty level.
Review filtersThe Review Queue can be filtered by domain, difficulty, and why the item needs review.

Answer Feedback

Feedback is edited for sufficiency rather than forced length. The app gives the direct reason, adds a topic-specific concept explanation when useful, discusses a wrong choice only when it helps distinguish the answers, and finishes with a concise exam takeaway.

WhyThe shortest direct reason the keyed answer is correct.
ConceptAppears only when the app has a genuinely topic-specific explanation that adds to the Why statement. There is no generic fallback text.
Wrong choiceAppears after an incorrect answer only when the selected distractor can be specifically distinguished from the correct answer; generic filler is omitted.
GraphicFor questions with a dedicated teaching diagram, the diagram appears directly in feedback and remains available in review.
Exam takeawayA broader rule or recognition cue to carry into later questions.

Confidence Ratings

ConfidentYou knew the concept and selected the answer deliberately.
Guessed / eliminatedYou got it right, but it stays review-worthy because mastery is uncertain.
Still weakYou want the concept to remain in the Review Queue.

Visual Coverage

The bank includes 30 distinct visual scenarios supporting 150 figure-based questions—about 23% of the 650-question bank. Visuals cover geologic maps, cross sections, stratigraphy, hydrogeology, engineering geology, geomorphology, seismic interpretation, mineral/resources, and site investigation.

Figure Design

Figures are intentionally simplified for exam-style interpretation. Unexplained decorative lines are avoided, contacts that matter to a question are labeled, and unconformities are shown as actual stratigraphic boundaries rather than floating symbols.

Answer-Choice Balance

Correct-answer positions are balanced across A–D within the main practice pools, including difficulty and domain × difficulty groupings. Dedicated FG exam forms are also balanced by domain so answer-letter patterns do not become a test-taking cue.

Question Bank

The app contains a 650-question learning bank across the eight ASBOG domains and four study levels, plus two dedicated 140-question FG practice-exam forms. Exam-form questions are reserved from ordinary practice and appear only in their assigned exam or in post-exam review. These are original study questions, not actual ASBOG examination items.

FG Practice Exams

Form A and Form B each contain 140 dedicated questions with the FG domain weighting built into the form. They do not show immediate correctness or explanations during the exam. You may navigate freely and flag items; grading occurs only after final submission.

The two forms are intended as assessment sets rather than routine drilling. Normal Practice mode draws from the separate 650-question learning bank. After an exam is submitted, missed exam items may appear in the Review Queue.

Quick Reference

The Quick Reference page contains high-yield material across all eight domains: field geology, minerals/rocks, stratigraphy, geomorphology, structure/tectonics, hydrogeology, engineering geology, and mineral/energy resources.
